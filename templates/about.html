MY STORY

Curious by nature, builder by choice.

I started with a simple question: how do websites actually work? That curiosity turned into late nights with HTML and CSS, then JavaScript, then Python and Django, and now into real products for real businesses.

Today I build everything from clean business websites to online stores and custom web apps. I care about the details people feel but rarely notice: pages that load fast, layouts that work on every phone, and code that is easy to grow later.

Alongside client work I'm studying Intermediate (FA - IT), so I'm always learning, and I bring that energy to every project.
